# The Ark — Product Context

## Vision
The Ark is a modern, joyful, and secure Children's Church Management System. It empowers church administrators, teachers, and volunteer attendants to seamlessly track attendance, conduct secure child check-in and guardian pick-up, organize class cohorts, and steward financial offerings.

## Target Audience & Usage Scene
- **Church Administrators & Ministry Directors:** Manage cohorts, monitor service-wide metrics, review attendance trends, and audit financial records.
- **Sunday School Teachers:** Quick roster review, view allergy/guardian safety notes, celebrate birthdays, track attendance in real-time.
- **Kiosk Volunteers & Parents:** High-speed check-in during peak arrival times (8:45 AM - 9:15 AM); strict safety verification during child pick-up (authorized guardian match).

## Surface Mode
- **Primary Mode:** `Operate`
- High scanability, rapid task completion, fail-safe child safety checks, zero visual clutter, and accessible contrast under bright church hall or auditorium lighting.
